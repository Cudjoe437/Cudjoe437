Sweet Crumb Bakery

Sweet Crumb Bakery is a family-owned artisan bakery in Austin, Texas that bakes custom wedding cakes, sourdough bread and gluten-free pastries fresh every morning since 2009.

Custom wedding cakes

Our custom wedding cakes are designed with each couple during a free tasting consultation. Custom wedding cakes start at $4 per slice and serve 50 to 300 guests.

Sourdough bread

Our sourdough bread uses a 15-year-old starter and a 36-hour fermentation for flavor and digestibility. Fresh sourdough bread is ready at 7am daily.

Gluten-free pastries

Gluten-free pastries are baked in a dedicated gluten-free oven to avoid cross-contact. We offer gluten-free pastries, cookies and cupcakes.
